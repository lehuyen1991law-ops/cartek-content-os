### Purpose and evidence per pillar

**P1 — Cartek 2 how-to.** Show how to use the tool for real diagnostic work. Evidence: every Cartek 2 capability needs a verified entry in the [feature register](../data/reference/cartek-2-feature-register.yaml) (product documentation or a recorded hands-on test). The register is empty today, so every `cartek-feature` claim is blocked by QC gate G3. All four P1 ideas depend on it.
- CTK-L-0001 promises vehicle coverage. Coverage is a compatibility claim: list only makes and models that were tested and recorded.
- CTK-L-0003 is about clearing codes. The script must say that clearing does not repair the cause; QC fails any wording that says otherwise.

**P2 — How car systems work.** Explain systems so viewers understand what the diagnostic data means. Evidence: textbooks, OEM service information, standards.
- CTK-L-0005 and CTK-L-0006: maintenance intervals and DIY steps differ by manufacturer and model. Cite the OEM schedule or procedure for each vehicle named. DIY repairs usually trigger safety flags (see [editorial guidelines](editorial-guidelines.md#safety-flagged-topics)).
- CTK-L-0007 (diesel): high-pressure diesel fuel systems are a fuel-system safety topic.
- CTK-L-0008 (garage equipment): names of third-party products are trademarks; any Cartek 2 claim needs the register.

**P3 — Common faults.** Help viewers act sensibly when a symptom or warning lamp appears. Evidence: standards for generic codes, OEM service information for causes and tests. A symptom or code is a starting point, not a confirmed root cause.
- CTK-L-0011 ("Top 5 lỗi..."): a ranking needs a source for "thường gặp". Without one, present the list as examples, not a ranking.
- CTK-L-0012 (Mercedes, BMW): vehicle-specific content. Name model, year range and engine, and cite OEM information for each claim. Manufacturer-controlled code ranges require `vehicle.scope: specific` (QC gate G5).

**P4 — OBD2 in depth.** Teach the OBD-II system itself. Evidence: SAE/ISO standards first, then OEM information and textbooks.
- CTK-L-0013: the "hộp đen" (black box) metaphor must not suggest that the OBD-II system is a crash or event recorder. Check the framing in research.
- CTK-L-0014: the P/B/C/U letters and the generic versus manufacturer-controlled code ranges come from SAE J2012 / ISO 15031-6. Cite the standard.
- CTK-L-0015: P0300 belongs to the generic, SAE-defined P0xxx range. See the recommendation below.
- CTK-L-0016: "personalising" the car through OBD2 can touch emissions, warranty and safety systems. It needs a technical review and must never suggest disabling emission or safety functions.

**P5 — News and trends.** Industry news relevant to Vietnamese garages. Evidence: dated news or trade sources; say clearly what is announced versus what is speculation. The sheet has no ideas for this pillar yet.

**S1 — Quick tips.** One useful action in under 60 seconds.
- CTK-S-0001 ("cách khắc phục nhanh chóng"): do not promise quick fixes for faults that need diagnosis.
- CTK-S-0002 ("Reset đèn check engine trong 2 phút"): the clearing caveat is mandatory, even in a Short. Consider framing it as "when it is safe to reset the lamp, and why the lamp comes back".

**S2 — Products and equipment.** Short product introductions. Cartek 2 claims need the register; third-party claims need the maker's documentation.
- CTK-S-0006 (fuel-saving accessories): fuel-economy claims need measured evidence; treat them as unverified until tested. If the Short shows work on fuel lines or fuel pressure, QC requires the `fuel-system` safety flag.

**S3 — Simple repairs.** Short how-to repairs a car owner can do.
- CTK-S-0007 (spark plugs) and CTK-S-0008 (air filter): procedures and torque values are model-specific; cite the OEM procedure. Spark plug work usually involves hot engine parts and ignition components, so decide the safety flags during research.
- CTK-S-0009 ("ì máy"): list possible causes as candidates with the test that confirms each, not as a diagnosis.

**S4 — Frequent questions.** Short answers to questions car owners ask.
- CTK-S-0010: telling viewers whether to keep driving is a safety statement and needs a standard or OEM source.
- CTK-S-0011 and CTK-S-0012: intervals depend on the manufacturer, the oil and the operating conditions. Do not give one universal number without a source; point viewers to their owner's manual.

### How to pick the next item

Use these criteria, in order:

1. **Evidence is reachable now.** Prefer topics that standards and OEM sources can support without the empty Cartek 2 feature register.
2. **Audience fit.** The topic solves a pain point listed in the sheet for the primary persona.
3. **Safety risk.** Lower-risk topics move faster; flagged topics need a qualified human reviewer.
4. **Demonstration needs.** The vehicle, tool and location must be available.

**Recommended first production item: CTK-L-0015, "Lỗi P0300 - Ác Mộng của Mọi Động Cơ?"** ('Content Direction'!F12, pillar P4, default persona technician).

- P0300 is in the generic P0xxx range defined by SAE, so its meaning can be cited from the standard and confirmed in OEM service information.
- The topic matches the technician pain point "xử lý sự cố phức tạp" ('Khán giả'!C4).
- The research can be completed without any Cartek 2 claim.
- Any demonstration segment that shows Cartek 2 still needs verified entries in the [feature register](../data/reference/cartek-2-feature-register.yaml). Until then, show the diagnostic data with neutral wording and without capability claims, or leave the demo out.
- Misfire diagnosis often involves ignition parts and fuel injectors. Decide the safety flags or dismissals during research.

Start it with:

```bash
python3 scripts/new_content.py --from-idea CTK-L-0015 --audience technician
```

Then follow the [idea-to-publish workflow](../workflows/idea-to-publish.md).
